# Starting baskets

Themes an owner may ask for by name, as tickers only. Every ticker here still goes
through step 1 of the procedure — one `bevo-read token-search` per ticker, the owner
picks the exact contract from the matches. A preset is a conversation starter, not a
list of addresses, and it is never a recommendation.

Equal weight unless the owner says otherwise.

## AI agents

VIRTUAL, AIXBT, VADER, LUNA, GAME

The liquid end of the agent tokens. Most live on Base. Several are thin outside the
top two, so step 2's `token-stats` check matters more here than anywhere else —
a 20% leg in a thin pool pays for it on every rebalance.

## Blue chip

ETH, SOL, BNB

Three chains' own coins, no contracts to resolve, deepest liquidity. The usual
answer for an owner who says "just keep it simple".

## Base ecosystem

VIRTUAL, AERO, BRETT, DEGEN

Everything on chain 8453. One chain means no bridging and the smallest spread
between what a rebalance sells and what it buys.

## Solana ecosystem

SOL, JUP, JTO, BONK

Chain 1151111081099710. The three contract legs are mints, not EVM addresses.

## Majors plus agents

ETH, SOL, VIRTUAL, AIXBT

A half-and-half for an owner who wants agent exposure without the whole basket
moving on one narrative. Weighting the two majors higher — 30/30/20/20 — is a
common ask and is worth offering.

## What is not here

No tokenized stocks and no perps: the duty is spot crypto only. No stablecoin leg
either — USDC is what the rebalance trades through, so holding it as a leg would
have the duty buying and selling its own cash.
